Welcome to the Collective: Meet Silvia Pina

By Garret McCann & Mapa Laverde · July 19, 2026 · Back to Blog

Big news from the collective: our team is growing. Please join us in welcoming Silvia Pina, the newest trainer at Quantic Wellness Collective.

In her own words:

“Hi, I’m Silvia and I’ve been in the wellness space for over 20 years. In that time, one of my greatest joys has been helping others step into their power both physically and emotionally. I just moved here from Miami and I’m so happy to be in Asheville. I’m even happier to be part of the Quantic Wellness community. I look forward to meeting you.”

Silvia is a 500-Hour Registered Yoga Teacher and Certified Personal Trainer with over two decades of teaching experience. Her approach bridges the worlds of mindful movement and functional strength, with a special focus on women’s health.

Whether guiding a breath-centered vinyasa or working one-on-one in a training session, she blends precision, intuition, and humor to help clients feel strong, aligned, and empowered. Her work emphasizes building lean muscle mass, increasing bone density, and enhancing functional strength to support lifelong vitality.

Silvia welcomes clients of all levels, creating a space for grounded connection to mind, body, and source.

Come say hello. We can’t wait for you to meet her.
Garret & Mapa
