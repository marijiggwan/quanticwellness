QUANTIC Grand Opening: A Love Letter to Asheville

By Garret McCann & Mapa Laverde · May 2, 2026 · Back to Blog

Some moments feel bigger than a business.
Bigger than a grand opening.
Bigger than a single day.

Our Grand Opening was one of those moments.

What filled the space that day was not just movement, music, food, or celebration. It was connection. It was community. It was humanity. It was a shared belief that wellness can be something deeper, something that brings people back home to themselves and to each other.

From the moment the doors opened, QUANTIC was alive in the most beautiful way imaginable.

Friends, family, clients, neighbors, new faces, children laughing and running through the studio, conversations between strangers becoming connections, music vibrating through the walls, movement flowing through every room. It all felt surreal. Like a dream we had carried in our hearts for a very long time finally taking physical form.

There were moments throughout the day where we simply paused and looked around in gratitude.

The energy in the room reminded us exactly why we chose to build QUANTIC.

Not simply to create another fitness space.
But to create a place where people feel seen.
Supported.
Inspired.
Empowered.

A place where wellness becomes community.
Where movement becomes healing.
Where strength exists alongside softness and humanity.

To every single person who showed up for us: thank you.

To our clients who believed in us before the walls were finished, before the signs were up, before the vision fully existed outside of our minds, thank you for trusting us.

To our friends and family who encouraged us through every stressful moment, every late night, every obstacle, every fear, every uncertain step, thank you for carrying this dream with us.

To the local businesses, collaborators, artists, wellness professionals, our DJ Rae, creators, and community members who continue to support what we are building, thank you for reminding us how powerful community truly is.

And to the children filling the space with joy and life that day: thank you for reminding all of us what wellness is really about. Life. Presence. Play. Connection. Family. The future.

Building QUANTIC has required more courage, sacrifice, resilience, and faith than words can fully explain.

There have been sleepless nights. Long days. Unexpected challenges. Moments where we had to keep moving forward purely because of how deeply we believed in this purpose living within our hearts.

And still, we would choose this path again and again.

Because QUANTIC was never built only for us.

It was built as a love letter to Asheville.
A love letter to human wellbeing.
A love letter to the possibility of what happens when people come together with intention, care, and a desire to make the world a little healthier, more connected, and more alive.

This is only the beginning.

We are endlessly grateful for every person walking beside us as this vision continues to grow.

Thank you for believing in QUANTIC.
Thank you for believing in community.
Thank you for being part of this story.

With gratitude,
Mapa & Garret
The QUANTIC Wellness Collective Team
