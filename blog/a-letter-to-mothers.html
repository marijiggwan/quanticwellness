Mother’s Day · May 2026

To Become a Mother Is to Be Transformed by Life Itself

By Mapa Laverde · May 10, 2026 · Back to Blog

Hi. I’m Mapa, co-founder of QUANTIC. As a single mother of two children, and a birth and postpartum doula, supporting women through motherhood is something profoundly personal to me. Motherhood has transformed every part of my life: my body, my mind, my emotions, my priorities, my resilience, and my understanding of strength itself.

Experiencing pregnancy, birth, postpartum depression, postpartum healing, and the constant evolution that comes with raising children has deepened my desire to create spaces where women feel supported, seen, empowered, and cared for through every stage of their journey. It is one of the reasons QUANTIC exists in the way it does today.

I know firsthand the courage it takes to rebuild yourself while simultaneously nurturing others. I know the vulnerability, the exhaustion, the reinvention, and also the immense power that motherhood awakens within women. Through QUANTIC, it is my deepest intention to give back to the mothers in our community by creating an environment where they can reconnect with themselves, rediscover their strength, and feel deeply supported physically, mentally, emotionally, and spiritually through movement, wellness, and community.

A woman does not simply give birth to a child.
She gives birth to new versions of herself over and over again.

Motherhood reshapes the body.
It expands the heart.
It deepens intuition.
It softens and strengthens simultaneously.
It asks women to surrender, to endure, to rebuild, to nurture, to protect, and somehow still continue rising through every season of life.

There is something profoundly cosmic about the ability to create life.

To carry a universe within your body.
To become a portal between worlds.
To feel your mind, emotions, spirit, identity, and physical body transform in ways words can barely explain.

And then… to begin again.

Again and again, mothers reinvent themselves.
More resilient.
More powerful.
More vulnerable.
More intuitive.
More alive.

At QUANTIC, this is something we deeply honor.

We see the courage it takes to reconnect with yourself after birth.
The patience required to heal.
The strength behind every sleepless night.
The endurance within every mother learning to care for herself while caring for others.

This is why we hold so much love and intention for the women in our community navigating pregnancy, postpartum, healing, rebuilding, and rediscovering themselves through movement and wellness.

This is why we intentionally design and tailor programming for pregnant and postpartum women with deep care, knowledge, and respect for the incredible transformations their bodies and nervous systems experience through motherhood.

Through functional movement, breathwork, mobility, strength training, nervous system support, recovery, and personalized guidance, we are here to walk beside mothers through every stage of their journey, helping them feel supported, empowered, safe, and deeply connected to themselves again.

To all mothers:
we see you.
we admire you.
we honor you.

Thank you for the life you create, the love you carry, and the strength you continue to embody every single day.

Happy Mother’s Day.
Mapa & Garret
