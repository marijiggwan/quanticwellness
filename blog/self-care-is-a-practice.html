International Self-Care Day · July 24

Self-Care Isn’t a Reward. It’s a Practice.

By Mapa Laverde · July 17, 2026 · Back to Blog

Self-care gets sold to us as something we earn. Push hard enough, get through the to-do list, and then you’re allowed to rest, to breathe, to take care of yourself.

We’d like to offer a different frame.

Self-care isn’t the treat at the end. It’s the foundation underneath everything else: the sleep that lets your body recover, the water you drink before you’re thirsty, the walk you take because your mind needs space, and the workout you show up for even when it’s a smaller version of what you planned. None of it needs to look impressive to count.

Wellbeing is a practice, not a finish line. That means there’s no version of you that has to “arrive” somewhere before self-care is warranted. You’re allowed to take care of yourself today, exactly as you are.

Think of July 24th less as a deadline and more as self-care’s birthday: a day to celebrate something that’s actually meant to live in the everyday, not just show up once a year. It doesn’t have to be big. It doesn’t have to be demanding. It just has to be there, and it’s okay to show up for yourself however you’re feeling in a given moment.

Consistency isn’t linear. It’s dynamic. Some days you have 100% to give, and giving yourself all of it is self-care. Other days you only have 30%, and giving yourself all of that 30% still counts. And some days your responsibilities take up nearly everything, leaving you with 5%. Offering yourself that 5% is enough too.

This July 24th, and every day of the year, we’re not asking you to overhaul anything. Just notice one place where you’ve been waiting for permission and give yourself that permission a little sooner than usual. Because waiting for permission can quietly become its own kind of demand.

This week at QUANTIC: however you move through our doors, for a class, a session, or just to say hi, that’s enough. We’re glad you’re here!
